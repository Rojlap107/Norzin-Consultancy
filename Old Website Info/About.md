## Content Summary
Norzin Consultancy is the brainchild of two Tibetans with extensive backgrounds in international development and business management.

**Key Experience:**
- Worked with major NGOs and CTA (Central Tibetan Administration).
- Involvement with Monlam AI, FNF, and USAID projects.
- Experience in financial management and organizational strategy.

**Mission:**
To empower small businesses and NGOs with the tools, strategies, and financial insights they need for sustainable growth.

**Vision:**
Combining modern business practices with local expertise to support Tibetan and Himalayan communities.
