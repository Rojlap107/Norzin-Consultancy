# Job Readiness Training - Dharamsala

**URL:** [https://norzinconsultancy.wordpress.com/2025/02/28/job-readiness-training-dharamsala/](https://norzinconsultancy.wordpress.com/2025/02/28/job-readiness-training-dharamsala/)
**Date:** February 28, 2025

## Content
In collaboration with YES+, Norzin Consultancy held a Job Readiness Training in Dharamsala on December 28 and 29, 2024.

The program was tailored for youth and job seekers, covering:
- Career planning and goal setting.
- Workforce entry strategies.
- Communication skills for professional environments.
- Practical exercises on job applications.

The initiative is part of an ongoing effort to support the economic empowerment of the community.
