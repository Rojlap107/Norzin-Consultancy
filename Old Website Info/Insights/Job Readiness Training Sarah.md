# Job Readiness Training at Sarah College

**URL:** [https://norzinconsultancy.wordpress.com/2025/02/28/job-readiness-training-sarah-college-for-higher-tibetan-studies/](https://norzinconsultancy.wordpress.com/2025/02/28/job-readiness-training-sarah-college-for-higher-tibetan-studies/)
**Date:** February 28, 2025

## Content
A two-day Job Readiness Training was conducted for the final-year students of Sarah: College for Higher Tibetan Studies on Feb 14 and 15, 2025. 

Supported by YES+ (Department of Home, CTA), the session equipped students with essential career tools, including:
- Professional resume and cover letter writing.
- Effective interview techniques.
- Interactive mock interview sessions.

The training focused on bridging the gap between academic learning and professional expectations.
