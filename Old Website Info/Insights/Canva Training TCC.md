# Canva Training at Tibetan Career Centre (TCC)

**URL:** [https://norzinconsultancy.wordpress.com/2025/02/28/canva-training-at-tibetan-career-centre-tcc/](https://norzinconsultancy.wordpress.com/2025/02/28/canva-training-at-tibetan-career-centre-tcc/)
**Date:** February 28, 2025

## Content
Norzin Consultancy organized a specialized Canva training session at the Tibetan Career Centre (TCC) on January 20 and 21, 2025.

The workshop was designed to help participants:
- Master professional design tools in Canva.
- Create visually compelling content for business and social media.
- Understand basic design principles for professional layouts.

This training aims to empower individuals with digital design skills necessary for modern workplaces.
