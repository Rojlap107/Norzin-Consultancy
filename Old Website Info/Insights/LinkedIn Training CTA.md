## Content
Norzin Consultancy conducted a specialized LinkedIn training for the staff of CTA’s Department of Home.

The training session focused on:
- Optimizing professional LinkedIn profiles.
- Strategies for professional networking and engagement.
- Leveraging LinkedIn for organizational visibility and outreach.
- Best practices for content sharing and professional branding.

This session was part of the consultancy's commitment to enhancing digital proficiency and professional networking capabilities within the Tibetan administration.
